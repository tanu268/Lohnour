# **1\. Company Overview**

## **What is Lohnour Pro?**

Lohnour Pro is an AI Training, Consulting, and Implementation company.

We help organizations and professionals move from **AI curiosity** to **AI capability**.

We believe AI adoption is not about buying more tools. It is about building the skills, workflows, and systems that help people do better work.

We are a **training and consultancy company first**, with AI as our initial focus. Over time, the company may expand into broader capability-building domains, but AI is our current specialization.

# **15\. Editorial Checklist**

Before publishing ask:

Is it useful?

Is it true?

Is it practical?

Does it teach something?

Does it sound like Lohnour Pro?

Would we be proud of this a year from now?

If the answer to any question is "No", revise before publishing.

## **Final Principle**

When you're unsure what to create, ask yourself:

> **Will this help someone move one step closer to confidently adopting AI?**

If the answer is **yes**, it's probably aligned with Lohnour Pro.

If the answer is **no**, rethink it.
