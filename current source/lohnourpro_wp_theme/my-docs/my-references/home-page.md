1. Hero

Goal: Make the visitor immediately understand what Lohnour Pro does and why they should care.

Contains:

Strong headline
Supporting copy
Primary CTA (Book Consultation)
Secondary CTA (Explore Programs)
Hero visual/illustration/animation
2. Trusted By / Social Proof

Even if you're just starting, leave space for it.

Eventually this can include:

Client logos
Workshop partners
Universities
Companies
Number of professionals trained

This section grows with the business.

3. What We Do

A concise explanation.

Not services.

Rather:

We help organizations and professionals adopt AI through practical, hands-on training.

Think of it as the "elevator pitch."

4. Training Areas (Services)

Your core offerings.

Example cards:

Corporate AI Training
Agentic AI Workshops
AI Productivity
Faculty Development Programs
Student Workshops

Each links to its own page.

5. Why Lohnour Pro?

This is where you differentiate yourself.

Examples:

Practical, not theoretical
Hands-on learning
Customized for your organization
Built around real workflows
Latest AI tools and techniques
6. Learning Experience / Process

Show people what working with you looks like.

Example:

Understand goals
Customize curriculum
Deliver workshop
Hands-on exercises
Post-training resources
Continued support

This builds trust because buyers can imagine the engagement.

7. Featured Programs / Workshops

Highlight current or flagship programs.

This section can pull dynamically from your Workshops CPT.

8. Case Studies / Success Stories

Show outcomes.

Examples:

Before/After
Metrics
Client stories
Workshop highlights

This can evolve as you build a portfolio.

9. Testimonials

Human proof.

Eventually include:

Photo
Name
Role
Company
Testimonial
10. Insights / Latest Articles

Automatically pull the latest blog posts.

This demonstrates expertise and supports SEO.

11. Final CTA

A strong closing section.

Examples:

Book a Consultation
Request Corporate Training
Let's Discuss Your AI Upskilling Goals
12. Footer

Not just links.

Include:

Brand summary
Navigation
Contact
Social links
Copyright
Legal pages

Why this order?

It mirrors how a buyer thinks:

Who are you?
Can I trust you?
What exactly do you do?
Is it relevant to me?
Why should I choose you?
How does it work?
What have you done before?
What do others say?
Do you know your subject?
How do I get started?
